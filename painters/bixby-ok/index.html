Bixby Is Still Growing, Fast

Bixby's population went from 13,336 at the 2000 census to 20,884 in 2010, to 28,617 by April 2020, to an estimated 33,515 in 2026, a 124.61% increase since 2000 that puts Bixby ahead of 91% of similarly sized U.S. cities on growth over that stretch. City Manager Jared Cottle has attributed the growth largely to the Bixby School District, and says the city adds an average of about 275 to 300 new single-family homes a year.

Bixby is not simply a repeat of Jenks next door. Natural gas was discovered nearby in 1905 and 1906 and oil followed in 1914, and the town's roots as an agricultural trade hub carried it through the Depression while other towns shrank. That older identity still shows up today in local events like the Bixby BBQ and Blues Festival and the Green Corn Festival.

Growth With Growing Pains

That pace has real, current consequences. In early 2026, Bixby residents filed a 396-signature petition opposing a rezoning near the Lantern Hill development, citing that all four Bixby elementary schools are already at capacity, that only one bridge provides vehicle access in and out of the city, and pointing to existing water-pressure problems. The rezoning vote was postponed after the developer requested changes. We mention this only as evidence of how fast Bixby is building, not as a claim about any specific property.

Subdivisions and Acreage: Two Different Jobs

Rowan Grove is Bixby's newest gated villa-style community, opened October 2025 at 131st and Mingo with homes starting from $360K. Robinson Ranch, near historic downtown Bixby, has 381 homes from $234K to $271K on 1,143 to 1,700 square feet, with a modest $21 per month HOA fee. Other named communities in our research include River Crest, Henson Square, and Torrey Lakes.

Unlike a fully built-out subdivision city, Bixby also retains a real acreage and rural-residential layer. Current listings include 5.77-acre, 10-acre, 28-acre, and 160-acre parcels marketed specifically as Bixby-schools acreage. Those properties typically bring detached outbuildings, barns, and fencing in addition to the home itself, a different job than a 1,143-square-foot tract home in Robinson Ranch.
