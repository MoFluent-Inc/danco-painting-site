Featured service

Cabinet Painting and Staining

Cabinet refinishing runs on prep, not paint. Doors get labeled and degreased, and their factory sheen gets stripped before primer goes on, since new paint won't grip an intact glossy finish.

Learn about cabinet painting and staining

Exterior Painting

A repaint starts with a wash and a real dry-out window before any scraping begins. Loose paint gets scraped to a stable edge, wood gets repaired, and gaps get caulked and primed before the topcoat goes on.

Learn more about exterior painting

Interior Painting

Every patched wall gets primed before the topcoat, not just painted over, since an unprimed patch dries a different sheen than the wall around it. Sheen is chosen room by room, not one default finish everywhere.

Learn more about interior painting

Epoxy Flooring

Concrete gets tested for internal moisture and ground to open its pores before any color goes down. Most epoxy floor failures trace back to skipped prep, not the coating itself.

Learn more about epoxy flooring

Power Washing

Pressure washing and soft washing are different tools for different surfaces. Concrete and brick take real force; painted siding, stucco, and roof shingles need a gentler, chemical-based soft wash instead.

Learn more about power washing
